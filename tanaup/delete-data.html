Delete your TanaUp data

TanaUp · by Swarte Interactive · Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short

  • You can delete all your TanaUp data yourself, inside the game, in a few seconds.
  • Or email us and we'll delete it for you within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the game alone does not delete your online data.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

  1. Open TanaUp on your phone.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then tap it again to confirm.

This deletes your data straight away and resets the game on your phone.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the game any more, send an email to swarteinteractive@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TanaUp data".

Please include your friend code (shown on the Friends screen in the game) or your gamer tag, so we can find your account. We'll delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your online player account (the random player ID)
  • Your gamer tag, avatar, and the country and province you picked
  • Your scores, records, league points, daily results and best-run "ghosts"
  • Your friends list and friend code
  • Your cloud-saved progress (gems, items, levels, settings)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Anonymous usage data (Firebase Analytics) is not linked to you, so it can't be picked out and deleted. Google keeps it for at most 14 months, then it's removed automatically.
  • Purchases stay linked to your Google account, so you can restore them later with "Restore purchases". Google Play keeps its own purchase records.
  • Ad data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy.
