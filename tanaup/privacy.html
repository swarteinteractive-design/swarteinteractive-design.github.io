Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Applies to the TanaUp game for Android

In short

  • We never ask for your name, email or phone number to play.
  • The game gives you a random player ID so your scores, leagues, friends and saved progress work online.
  • If you choose a country and province, they are shown with your scores. We never use GPS or your exact location.
  • Usage statistics and personalised ads are only used if you agree. You can change your mind any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete your data any time: Settings → Delete my data, or see Delete your TanaUp data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Pascal Swarte, trading as Swarte Interactive, an independent game developer based in Spain.

3. Do you have to give us this data?

No law makes you give us any data. The random player ID is needed for the online parts of the game (rankings, friends, cloud save). The gamer tag, avatar, country and province are optional. Usage statistics and personalised ads only happen if you agree, and the game works the same if you say no.

4. Who handles the data for us

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database and Analytics) works for us as a data processor under Google's data processing terms. Our game database is stored in Google's data centre in Madrid, Spain. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob shows the ads. For ads, Google also uses data for its own purposes, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Play (Billing and Play Games Services) handles purchases and Play Games sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy.

Your gamer tag, avatar, chosen country and province, and game records are shown to other players on leaderboards. Your saved progress is private. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

5. Data sent outside the EU

Google may process some data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, so your data stays protected.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we have about you (access);
  • correct it if it's wrong (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure) — see Delete your TanaUp data;
  • limit how we use it (restriction);
  • get a copy to take elsewhere (portability);
  • object to uses based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email swarteinteractive@gmail.com. Please include your friend code (shown on the Friends screen) or gamer tag so we can find your data. We answer within one month.

Usage statistics are linked only to a random app ID that the game doesn't show you or us, so we usually can't tell which statistics are yours. In that case we can't pick them out to show or delete them (GDPR art. 11). They are deleted automatically after at most 14 months.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid), or to the authority in your own country.

7. No automated decisions

We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly important effects on you. Rankings and leagues simply sort players by their scores.

8. Children

TanaUp is meant for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger players. If you think someone under 16 has used the game without a parent's permission, email us and we will delete the data.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each player's online records can only be changed by that player, and only we can access the game database.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the game.
