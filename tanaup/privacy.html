Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Applies to the TanaUp game for Android

In short

  • We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game gets a random player ID so your scores, leagues, friends and saved progress can work online.
  • If you choose a country and province, they are shown with your scores for the country and local rankings. We never use GPS or your exact location.
  • We collect anonymous information about how the game is played (for example which levels players reach) to make the game better.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EU and UK you choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete all your data in the game: Settings → Delete my data.

Who we are

TanaUp is made by Swarte Interactive, an independent game developer based in Spain. We are responsible for the data described here.

Your gamer tag, avatar, chosen country and province, and game records are public inside the game: other players can see them on leaderboards. Your saved progress is private.

Ads and your choices

TanaUp shows optional rewarded video ads (for example to get a second chance). They are served by Google AdMob. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Google's use of data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Analytics): Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services: Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your online game data while you play. If your player ID is not used for 24 months, we may delete its data. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the game.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings and tap Delete my data twice. This removes your online records, friends, saved progress and online account, and resets the game on your phone. Anonymous usage data is not linked to you and is kept by Google Analytics for at most 14 months. Things you bought stay linked to your Google account and can be restored with "Restore purchases".

You can also email us at swarteinteractive@gmail.com with your friend code (shown in the Friends screen) and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Step-by-step guide: Delete your TanaUp data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and you can object to how we use it. Email us to do this. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Children

TanaUp is meant for players aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger players. If you think someone under 16 has used the game without a parent's permission, email us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Online records can only be changed by the player they belong to.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the game.
