Help

Questions and support

Is TanaUp free?

Yes. TanaUp is free to play. You can earn gems by playing. Optional gem packs and extras can be bought through Google Play, and ads only play when you choose to watch one.

I changed phones. How do I get my progress back?

Open TanaUp on your new phone and choose "Load my progress" when asked. Things you bought can be restored with "Restore purchases" in the shop, using the same Google account.

How do I delete my data?

In the game, go to Settings and tap "Delete my data". You can also email us. Step by step: Delete your TanaUp data.

Something went wrong with a purchase.

Refunds go through Google Play (Order history). If an item didn't arrive or doesn't work, email swarteinteractive@gmail.com with your friend code and we'll make it right.

Who can play?

TanaUp is meant for players aged 16 and over. Younger players need a parent's or guardian's permission.
