The studio

Independent by design.

We're a small, independent studio from Castellón, on Spain's Mediterranean coast. Staying small means we answer to our players, not to a big publisher.

It also means every detail gets attention: how a game feels in your hands, how it sounds, how fast it loads, and how fair it is to play.

  • Independent and player-focused
  • Free to play, never pay-to-win
  • Built for players around the world

How we work

Fun first. Then everything else.

Find the fun

Every game starts as a small, rough prototype. If it isn't fun with plain shapes, it never will be. So we keep playing until it is.

Polish every detail

Then come the feel, sound, art and speed. We test on many kinds of phones and in many languages, not only the newest devices.

Listen and grow

After launch we keep listening. Player feedback shapes new content, new modes and our next games.
