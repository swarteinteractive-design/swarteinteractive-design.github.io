About us

Small studio.
Big ideas.

Swarte Interactive is an independent game studio based in Castellón, on Spain's sunny Mediterranean coast.

Our story

Born from a love of games.

Swarte Interactive started with a simple idea: make the kind of games we love to play ourselves. Original, polished and fair, and fun for the whole family.

Our first game, TanaUp, is named after the little girl who inspired it. It won't be the last.

We're a small team, which means every detail gets real attention. From the first sketch to the final release, we make games we'd be proud to hand to our own kids.
